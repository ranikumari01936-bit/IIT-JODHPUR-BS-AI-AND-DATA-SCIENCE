House Price Prediction Outputs

1. Predicting a numeric house price (e.g., ₹65,00,000) -> Regression
2. Predicting another numeric house price (e.g., ₹82,50,000) -> Regression
3. Predicting whether a house belongs to a price category (Low/Medium/High) -> Classification

Conclusion:
Specific house price prediction is a supervised regression task because the target is a continuous numerical value.
